What is margin debt?

Margin debt is money investors borrow from brokers to buy stocks. When margin debt grows rapidly, it signals increasing leverage and risk-taking. When it falls, investors are reducing exposure.

Why it matters:

  • Rising margin debt = investors confident, taking more risk
  • Peak margin debt = often precedes market tops (2000, 2007, 2021)
  • Falling margin debt = deleveraging, often near bottoms

How to read this chart
